Heart-rate limits

These numbers matter. The defaults are general estimates for your age, not medical advice. Ask your doctor for your own limits, especially if you take heart or blood-pressure medication (for example beta-blockers), which change heart rate. Choosing a zone or typing a max heart rate fills in the target and ceiling from it; you can still change them. Estimated limits follow your birthdays automatically; limits you type in yourself are never changed.

Phone reminders

Install the free ntfy app on your phone and subscribe to a topic name only you know (e.g. row-k7f2q9). Enter the same name here. Leave it empty to turn reminders off. Reminder text is generic and passes through the ntfy server.

Videos

For the Videos button at the top. Paste YouTube playlist links, one per line (open a playlist on YouTube and copy the address; it contains list=). Each session picks one of them and plays it shuffled, with sound. Videos that don't allow playing outside YouTube are skipped.

Diagnostics

Records what passes between the app and the monitor (connection events, raw USB data, rowing and heart-rate data, errors) until you stop it. Start it, reproduce the problem, then stop it. The file is saved in ~/rowing-backups/diagnostics and copied to Google Drive with the backups.
