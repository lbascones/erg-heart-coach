Heart-rate limits

These numbers matter. The defaults are general estimates for your age, not medical advice. Ask your doctor for your own limits, especially if you take heart or blood-pressure medication (for example beta-blockers), which change heart rate. Estimated limits follow your birthdays automatically; limits you type in yourself are never changed.
